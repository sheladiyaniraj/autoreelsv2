## Why is CapCut banned in India?

CapCut is developed by ByteDance — the same company behind TikTok. In June 2020, following border tensions with China, the Indian government banned TikTok and 58 other Chinese-owned apps under Section 69A of the Information Technology Act, citing threats to data privacy, national security, and India's sovereignty. CapCut wasn't in that first batch, but the government kept adding Chinese apps to the block list in later notifications, and CapCut was banned in India in 2023 as part of that continued crackdown — again citing the same underlying concern: that user data collected by ByteDance apps could be accessed by the Chinese government under Chinese law.

Practically, that means:

- CapCut isn't available on the Play Store or App Store for Indian accounts.
- Indian ISPs block access to CapCut's servers, so even a sideloaded copy is unreliable.
- The ban is a standing government order, not a temporary outage — there's no official timeline for it to be lifted.

## Can you still use CapCut in India?

Technically, some people get around this with a VPN or a sideloaded APK from outside the Play Store. We'd steer clear of that route: sideloaded APKs don't get security updates, a VPN doesn't change the fact that your data is still flowing to the same banned app, and you're relying on an unofficial file from a source you can't fully vet. It's also just an ongoing hassle — VPN on, VPN off, hope the sideload still works after the next update. If the entire concern that got CapCut banned in the first place was data privacy, routing around the block doesn't actually solve that problem — it just makes you the exception the ban was trying to close.

The more sustainable move is switching to a tool that's fully legal in India and doesn't leave you dependent on workarounds.
